### 1. doesn't it?

- 音标：/ˈdʌzənt ɪt/
- 类型：tag question
- 标签：phrasal verb、communication、informal
- 形式：doesn't it?、doesn't it

- 释义：不是吗？ / It is a tag question used in English to confirm or seek agreement about something that was just stated. It often implies that the speaker expects the listener to agree with them.
  - 例：这个方案很不错，不是吗？ / This solution works well, doesn't it?

同义表达：
  - Right?：对吗？
  - Isn't it?：不是吗？
  - Don't you think?：你不觉得吗？

相关表达：
  - ain't it?：不是吗？

解析：
- ain't it 在口语中很常见，但不是书面用语。

原文例句：
