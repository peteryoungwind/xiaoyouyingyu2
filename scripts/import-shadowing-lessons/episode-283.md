Even though I usually travel with a big backpack and a sling bag, I still want to have an actual backpack for hikes, day trips, or exploring new cities. And because this one folds down to such a tiny size, it's a no-brainer of a pack—disappears in your main bag until you actually need it. The Osprey Day Pack folds into its own pocket, weighs almost nothing, and still gives you enough space for a jacket, snacks, camera gear, or a bottle of water. The straps are surprisingly comfortable for how light it is, and the material feels far more durable than you'd expect from something that small. What makes it a secret essential is how much freedom it adds. You can leave your main bag in the hostel, walk around hands-free, and still carry everything you need for the day. Most travelers underestimate how big of a difference that makes, but after one trip with it, you'll never go back to dragging your full pack everywhere again.
