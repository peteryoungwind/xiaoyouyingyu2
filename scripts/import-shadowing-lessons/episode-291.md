Map and prioritize your goals. Slow and steady wins the race. Create an inspiration board, and pin pictures and quotes to it that reflect the lifestyle you're striving for. Take a look at it every day to keep track of your progress. Invest in a planner or bullet journal as well if it makes organizing more fun. You might experience setbacks and fail plenty of times before you meet your goals, but the key thing is to adapt a resilient mindset.
