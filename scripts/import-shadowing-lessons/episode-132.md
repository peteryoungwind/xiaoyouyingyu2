## 五、口头填空 及时巩固地道表达

页面提示：口头填空后可以复习视频并关掉字幕，检查自己是否能够完全听懂。完全掌握才能在表达时脱口而出！

英文全文：

Let's finally pack my personal item. I like to keep either a medium sized bag or a tiny bag or both as my personal item. This one has a little attachable pouch. So I'm putting in my wallet, my portable phone charger essential, and my passport. Then I'm clipping that into the bag. I also will never ever go on a plane without my noise canceling headphones. These are genius because they block out all the noise around you. So crying babies, people arguing, whatever happens, you won't be hearing any of it. It's amazing. I put that into my bag along with my toiletries, my water bottle, and my book. And that is it for the bag.
