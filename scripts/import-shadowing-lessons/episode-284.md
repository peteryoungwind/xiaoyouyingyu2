A shoe pouch, and the one I've been using is from Peak Design. It sounds simple—once you've packed a pair of sandy beach shoes or sweaty running trainers next to your clean clothes, you instantly get why this thing matters. For beach vacations, it keeps sand out of your luggage. For runners or gym-goers, it separates your workout gear from everything else. And now that we're heading into the colder months when shoes get muddy or wet, it becomes even more essential. The Peak Design Shoe Pouch folds completely flat when empty and expands when you need it. It's made from a lightweight, weather-resistant 70D nylon with a polyurethane coating, so it's both water-repellent and easy to wipe clean. The fabric feels premium but tough, thin enough to stay compact yet strong enough to handle heavy shoes without tearing. Even stands upright on its own, which makes packing and unpacking way easier than using plastic bags. I've tested a few different versions over the years, but this one just nails the balance between durability, flexibility, and packability. It's one of those pieces of gear that quietly keeps your entire setup cleaner, lighter, and better organized, especially when you're moving between climates or activities on the same trip.
