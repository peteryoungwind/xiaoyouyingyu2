## 二、对照英文 排查卡壳的地方

页面提示：对照母语者的表达，查看不会和卡壳的地方

Tip No. 2: Speak out loud. Don't only listen, don't only read. You must speak English to improve your speaking. Speak out loud every day, talk to yourself in English. Yes, it may feel strange at first, but it really works. You can describe the room around you, you can talk about what you're doing, you can share a memory or story from your life, you can say your thoughts in English. For example: I'm cooking dinner now, I'm making pasta, it smells good. Or yesterday I went to the park, as so many people, the weather was nice. It doesn't matter if you make mistakes, just speak. This helps your brain get used to making English sentences, it also helps you feel more confident. You will stop being afraid to speak, you will learn how to think in English. You can do this in your room, in your car, or when you walk—anytime, anywhere. So remember, speak out loud every day, even 5 minutes helps.
