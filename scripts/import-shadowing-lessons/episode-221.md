## 二、对照英文 排查卡壳的地方

页面提示：对照母语者的表达，查看不会和卡壳的地方

I actually started eating chicken salad after moving to the United States and I've loved it ever since. I think it's just such a versatile dish and there's so many ways to enjoy it. You can turn it into wraps, salads, or sandwiches so you never get bored because this meal plan is for someone who likes variety in their diet. Make sure to cool down the chicken before starting to shred it. I just use two forks and I just shred it until it has a stringy texture. I add it to a bowl along with the veggies like celery, red onion, cranberries and some mayonnaise. If you want a lighter fat option, Greek yogurt works too, but I personally love the rich flavor that mayo brings.
