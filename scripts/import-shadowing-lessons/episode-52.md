- 标签：adj.、collocation、situation、communication purpose
- 形式：entire、the entire days、running an entire week

- 释义：整个的；整体的；全部 / Refers to something whole, complete, or with all its parts included.
  - 例：全世界都受到气候变化的影响。 / The entire world is affected by climate change.

同义表达：
  - whole：全部的
  - complete：完整的

相关表达：
  - the entire day：全天
  - the entire year：全年
  - the entire time：全程
  - the entire family：全家
  - from start to finish：从头到尾

- 原文中的表达来自自然口语，可结合例句记忆使用场景和搭配。
