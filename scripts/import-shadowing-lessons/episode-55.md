- 音标：/ə ˈkʌpəl deɪz/
- 类型：collocation
- 标签：collocation、time expression、informal
- 形式：a couple days

- 释义：几天 / To describe a short, unspecified period of time
  - 例：我们已经等了几天，等客户的回复。 / We've been waiting for a couple days to hear from the client.

同义表达：
  - a few days：几天
  - several days：几天

- 音标：/ɡreɪ/ /ˈɡluːmi/
- 类型：collocation
- 标签：collocation、weather、emotion、descriptive
- 形式：gray and gloomy

- 释义：灰蒙蒙；阴沉沉的 / Refers to a situation or environment that is dull, overcast, or depressing
  - 例：听到坏消息后，房间里的气氛变得灰暗沉闷。 / After hearing the bad news, the mood in the room became gray and gloomy.

同义表达：

  - gloomy：depressing

相关表达：
  - under the weather：不舒服；心情不好
  - bright and sunny：阳光明媚

- gray原意"灰色"； gloomy原意"阴暗；阴沉"；gray and gloomy通常用于描述天气、情绪或氛围，指阴沉、灰暗的状态，令人感到压抑或沮丧。
