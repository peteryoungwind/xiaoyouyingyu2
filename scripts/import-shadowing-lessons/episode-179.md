Starting off with what I wear for hiking, backpacking, and camping. First off, we have tops. I have my Uniqlo Heat Tech base layer in both black and white. This is really helpful when it's a lot colder and I need just that skin-tight base layer. I'll even use this when it's just cold in the city and I need something to stay a little extra warm. If we're going somewhere, where let's say it's 30 or 40 degrees fahrenheit, definitely when there's snow, usually I'll wear my Smartwool mid-layer. Then, I have my Columbia long sleeve, which I can use as a base layer or just as a top on its own. I wear this all the time, and you'll probably see this in most of my hiking or backpacking camping videos.
