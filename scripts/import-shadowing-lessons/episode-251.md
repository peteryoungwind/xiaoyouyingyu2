So for example, if your love language is words of affirmation, like you love being kind to people, giving people compliments, you feel like that's the way that you show people love, you have to make sure for yourself that you're one, speaking to yourself super kindly, outwardly and also just like in your own mind. Journaling was one of the things that they recommended, also speaking kindly about others to others. And after reading this I realize that when all those things are done or when I do those things for myself, I'm always feeling my best.
