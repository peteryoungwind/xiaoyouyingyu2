I do this right on the sheet tray and not in a bowl to minimize the dishes that I'm using as much as possible. Next, I'll salt and pepper the top side of the salmon as well. I'm not going to spend any time cutting off the skin before I bake it, because it's actually super easy to peel off once it's cooked and cooled down.
