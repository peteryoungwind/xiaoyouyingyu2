Why are more older Americans getting divorced?

为什么越来越多的美国老年人选择离婚？

★★★★★ 670 词 纽约时报

Americans age 50 and older are getting divorced more than ever, with almost half of all broken marriages in recent years coming from aging Gen Xers and Baby Boomers.

在美国，50岁及以上人群的离婚现象比以往任何时候都更为普遍，近年来，在所有婚姻破裂案例中，近半数都来自步入中老年的 X 世代与婴儿潮一代。

Nearly 40% of all divorces nationwide are among couples 50 years and older — separations known as “gray divorces.”

全美约 40% 的离婚发生在 50 岁及以上的夫妻之间——这类离异被称为“银发离婚”。

And though divorce has been dropping in most age groups across America, it has not among people 50 years and older.

与此同时，尽管美国大多数年龄群体的离婚率呈下降趋势，但 50 岁及以上人群却未见回落。
