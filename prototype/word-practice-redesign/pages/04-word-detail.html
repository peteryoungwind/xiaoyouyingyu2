adjust

/əˈdʒʌst/
释义
v. 适应；调整

to change something slightly so that it works better or fits a new situation.

例句
I need time to adjust to the new routine. 我需要时间适应新的日常安排。
常用搭配

adjust to a new environment · adjust your schedule · make small adjustments

来源：如何适应新环境
