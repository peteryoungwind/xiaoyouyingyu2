消费习惯

教学模式 初级 剩余 30 2/12

说第一句吧

可以用英文，也可以先中英混合表达。AI 会接住你的表达，再帮你变自然。

I usually spend money on...
我想聊一下网购
Ask me a question

I often buy small things online, but sometimes I don't really need them.

That is very common. What usually makes you click “buy now”?

✨ 教学点评

表达很清楚。可以把 “small things” 换成更具体的 “little impulse purchases”。

I sometimes make little impulse purchases online.

Maybe discounts and free shipping.
